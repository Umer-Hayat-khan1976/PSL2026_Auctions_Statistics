# PSL 2026 Auction - Chart Insights

Insights for the full dataset (135 player records, all teams and roles). Prices are in PKR lakh.
The app generates the same kind of bullets live under each chart and updates them with the sidebar filters.

## 1. Player Records by Role
- **Bowler** is the largest group with **54 records** (40% of all records).
- **Batsman** follows with **40** (30%), then **All-rounder** with **28** (21%).
- **Wicketkeeper-Batsman** is the smallest group with **13 records** (10%).
- Bowlers outnumber batsmen 54 to 40.

## 2. Average Recorded Sold Price by Role
- **All-rounder** has the highest average at **324 lakh**, followed by **Batsman** (310 lakh), **Bowler** (235 lakh) and **Wicketkeeper-Batsman** (228 lakh).
- Batsmen average 310 lakh but have a median of only 110 lakh, so a few expensive stars pull the average up.
- The most expensive record is **Steve Smith** (Batsman, direct signing) at **1,400 lakh**, followed by **Saim Ayub** at 1,260 lakh.
- Direct signings average 728 lakh, against 214 lakh for players bought at auction.

## 3. Base Price vs Recorded Sold Price
- Base and sold prices show a **strong positive relationship** (correlation **0.88**).
- **64%** of players sold above their base price and **36%** sold exactly at base.
- The biggest jump is **Khawaja Nafay** (Quetta Gladiators): **5.9x** his base price. **Ubaid Shah** (4.5x) and **Asif Afridi** (4.0x) follow.
- The median player sold for **1.29x** the base price.
- **7 records** have no base price (the direct signings) and are excluded from this chart.
